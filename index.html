Cop Trap

A turn-based cops & robber puzzle by CLOUDIT

Drag a cop one step at a time, then watch the thief run. Tackle it, or corner it so it can't move, before it reaches the red exit. Every case has a guaranteed solution.

Support

Found a bug, stuck on a case, or have an idea for new cases? Write to us at mustafa@cloudits.co. Please include your device model and the case number.

Delete your data

Cop Trap has no accounts. Your progress is stored only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game. To delete the analytics data linked to your installation:

  • In the game: on the title screen, tap Privacy & data, then Delete my analytics data. This also turns analytics off.
  • By email: write to mustafa@cloudits.co with the subject "Cop Trap data deletion".

Advertising partners handle their data under their own policies; you can reset or delete your device's advertising ID in your device settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Frequently asked questions

How do I move a cop?
Press a cop: the dots it can reach light up. Drag it onto one of them and let go. Only that cop moves; the others stay put.

What do the stars mean?
Finish a case in the fewest possible moves for three stars, within two extra moves for two.

I'm stuck.
Use Hint to see the best next move, or Undo to take back a step. The first hints are free; later ones can be unlocked with an optional short ad.

Will there be more cases?
Yes. New cases arrive automatically, without an app update.

How do I change my privacy choices?
On the title screen, tap Privacy & data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
